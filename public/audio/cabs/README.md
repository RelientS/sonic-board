# Cabinet impulse responses

Measured guitar-cabinet impulse responses, trimmed (to the point holding
99.995% of the energy, max 250 ms) with a short raised-cosine fade. Mono,
48 kHz, 24-bit PCM.

## mesa-2x12-v30/

Mesa 2x12 V30 impulse responses by Dark Days — https://darkdays.net (CC BY 4.0).
Closed-back 2x12, Celestion Vintage 30, Sennheiser MD421. Files are named
`<cap|cone|edge>-<grille|1in|2in>.wav`. Modified: trimmed and faded.
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

## marshall-4x12-greenback/

Emerald Pack 1.0 by Jester Dyne Productions — https://jester-dyne-productions.com (CC0).
1998 Marshall 1960AX 4x12, Celestion G12M Greenback. mic-1..3 and mic-5 are
SM57, mic-4 and mic-6 are e606 (mic-1..4 on the top-left speaker, mic-5..6 on
the bottom-left), in pack order.
